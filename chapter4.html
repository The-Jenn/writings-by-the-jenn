Lisa closed the front door and leaned against it for a moment. It felt entirely different than it had a few hours ago. The silence used to feel safe, now it just felt hollow.

She walked straight to the kitchen and pulled the bottle of bourbon from the cabinet. She poured a generous amount into a glass, walked over to the dining table, and sat in one of the chairs.

As the adrenaline of the evening finally began to calm and a wave of guilt washed over her. She stared at her drink. For the last four hours, she hadn't thought about Becky once. Not a single passing thought. Her mind had been consumed by the intensity of Carla's eyes, and the dangerous closeness between them in that bar.

She tossed back the bourbon. She looked down at her watch. It was 2am. With the time difference, that meant it was 9:00 PM in the States.

Lisa pulled her phone from her pocket and dialed her wife's number.

Becky picked up. The background noise was clatter of heavy silverware, loud voices and chatter of teenagers.

"Lisa! Hey!" Becky practically yelled. "Sorry, it's so loud in here. We're at the hotel restaurant having a big group dinner with all the students and the chaperones."

"It's fine, I can hear you," Lisa said. "How was the museum today?"

"Oh, it was wonderful. Betsy actually put her phone away for a whole hour to look at the exhibits," Becky laughed. "What about you? What have you been up to tonight? Enjoying the peace and quiet?"

Lisa looked at the empty bourbon glass.

"Yeah," Lisa lied. "Just had a quiet night in. I brought some case files home and caught up on paperwork. Ate some leftover pasta."

"Well, don't work too hard. You're supposed to be taking a break," Becky said. "Listen, I've got to run, they're bringing out the main courses. I love you."

"Love you too," Lisa whispered. "Have a good night."

The line went dead. Lisa set the phone face down on the table. The guilt was a bad feeling.

She stood up, checked the locks on the front door, and walked up the stairs to the bedroom. She didn't turn on the lamps as she stripped off the blazer and the black V-neck, and her pants tossing them onto the armchair in the corner.

She climbed into the large, empty bed and pulled the covers up, staring up at the dark ceiling.

She needed to stop this. She was a detective. She was a wife. She was a mother. She couldn't blow up her entire life just because the woman next door looked at her like she was the only thing that mattered. She needed to pull back and put the walls back up.

But every time Lisa closed her eyes, she didn't see the safe, predictable life she had built. She saw Carla, standing under the streetlamp, laying by the pool, shoulder to shoulder with her at the bar. Smiling that perfect smile.

The attraction wasn't just a spark anymore it was dangerous. And as Lisa lay awake in the dark, she realized she was going to have to fight like hell if she didn't want to get caught up in it.

——-

Lisa woke up the next morning to the sounds of birds chirping, and the sound of a heavy splash of someone doing a cannonball next door.

She rolled over, groaning as she rubbed her eyes. It was Saturday. Her head throbbed slightly from the bourbon, and her chest still felt tight from the guilt of her phone call with Becky. She dragged herself out of bed and walked over to the window, cracking the blinds just enough to see out.

Carla’s backyard had been transformed into a daytime club. The music was loud and the patio was filled with a group of ridiculously beautiful men and women. But even in a crowd of gorgeous people, Carla was the center of attention.

She was wearing a tiny black bikini, her hair wet, and she was standing by the edge of the pool holding a red plastic cup. She threw her head back and downed the drink as everyone cheered.

Lisa watched as Carla stumbled slightly, laughing as she made her way over to a lounge chair and threw herself down. A guy in board shorts followed her. He sat down on the edge of her lounger, leaned down, and kissed her.

Lisa’s stomach turned with jealously or envy she wasn’t sure.

But Carla only let it happen for a split second. She put her hands on the guy’s chest and shoved him hard. He completely lost his balance, falling backward and crashing into the pool with big splash. Everyone laughed, and Carla sat up, grinning.

Then, she turned her head back and looked directly up at Lisa’s window.

Lisa jerked back into the corner. Carla was smiling. She knew Lisa was watching.

Determined to shake off the effect the woman had on her, Lisa threw on a pair of old denim shorts and a t-shirt. She needed to ground herself. She needed dirt under her fingernails and a task to focus on. She grabbed her gardening gloves and headed out to the backyard to start planting the summer vegetables she and Becky had bought weeks ago.

By early afternoon, Lisa was covered in sweat. Her knees were stained with damp soil, her hair was sticking to her forehead, and her muscles ached in a satisfying way.

"Detective. Would you like to come for a swim?"

Lisa froze, her hands buried in the dirt. She looked up.

Carla was leaning over the top of the fence, resting her chin on her crossed arms. Her sunglasses were pushed up into her hair, and her eyes were a little glassy. She was clearly tipsy.

"I probably shouldn't," Lisa said, wiping her forehead with the back of her dirty arm. "I have to finish this."

"Oh, come on," Carla pouted playfully. "Let your hair down and live a little. You're working too hard."

Before Lisa could answer, a guy, the same one who had been shoved into the pool earlier, appeared behind Carla, wrapping an arm loosely around her waist. "Yeah, come over and hang out," he yelled over the fence, giving Lisa a friendly, drunken wave.

She shoved his arm off her waist. "Back off will you," she snapped She looked back at Lisa. "Everyone wants to meet the detective, Detective," Carla slurred.

Lisa stood up. She looked at Carla, she smiled at Lisa. Lisa couldn't help it, she smiled back.

"Go on," Carla said again, her voice dropping into a flirty tone that sent a tingle down Lisa's spine. "Get your suit on. Come join me... us."

Twenty minutes later, Lisa was walking around the front of her house, nervous. She was wearing black two piece swimsuit under a sheer white cover up. Every step toward Carla’s open side gate felt like crossing a point of no return.

The moment Lisa stepped onto the patio, Carla was there.

"You made it," Carla said, pressing a cold margarita into Lisa's hand. She didn't give Lisa a chance to protest before throwing an arm around Lisa's shoulders, pulling her against her side. Her skin was hot from the sun.

"Everyone," Carla yelled over the music, demanding the patio's attention. "This is my neighbor, Lisa. She's a lesbian cop, so everyone behave."

Lisa almost choked on her drink. A few of the guys whooped, clearly loving the introduction, while the women looked Lisa up and down with curiosity. Carla didn't let go of her. She paraded Lisa around the patio, introducing her to writers, editors, and friends from the city, showing her off with a possessive pride that felt like they were dating.

Lisa was nervous and on edge in the loud crowd, but Carla kept the drinks coming. By her third margarita, the tequila had melted away Lisa’s nervousness.

"In the pool," Carla demanded, tugging on Lisa's hand.

Lisa dropped her cover up onto a chair and slid into the pool. Carla slipped in right beside her, the water level hitting them just below the shoulders.

The pool was crowded, forcing everyone close together. A few of Carla's friends swam over, immediately bombarding Lisa with questions.

"So is it a dangerous job? Do you love it?"

Lisa laughed. "It's mostly paperwork, honestly. But yeah, I love it. It's about solving the puzzle."

As Lisa talked, answering their questions, she felt Carla. Carla had drifted closer. Carla’s bare leg brushed against Lisa’s thigh.

Lisa’s tensed up, but instead of pulling away, she did the exact opposite. She slowly, deliberately slid a fraction of an inch closer, until the side of her leg was pressed firmly against Carla's.

Carla wasn't listening to the conversation anymore. She was staring at Lisa's profile. Watching her lips. Being drunk was stripping away whatever thin layer of self restraint Carla had left. Her eyes were fixed on the movement of Lisa's lips as she spoke, her wet neck and the line of her collarbone. The hunger in Carla's gaze was so obvious, so intensely focused, that Lisa could physically feel it.

Carla wanted her. Badly. And looking at the flush creeping up Lisa's neck, she knew the detective wanted her right back.

———

Evening rolled around and the party died out.

The loud music was gone, replaced by the slow music and crickets. Carla’s friends had either piled into Ubers or were passed out in the guest rooms upstairs.

Lisa was still there.

The tequila buzz had faded into an exhaustion. She was shivering slightly, her black two piece still damp. She grabbed a half empty bottle of Corona and a crushed red plastic cup from the edge of the pool, tossing them into a black trash bag.

"You don't have to do that, you know," Carla said.

Lisa turned. Carla was standing by the sliding glass doors. She had wrapped a thick, white towel around her waist, but the top half of her black bikini was still on. She looked tired.

"I don't mind," Lisa said, tying off the trash bag. "I'm used to cleaning up messes."

Carla walked over. She picked up a dry towel from a lounge chair and held it out. "Here. You're shivering, Detective."

Lisa took the towel. She quickly wrapped it around her shoulders, pulling it tight across her chest. "Thanks. It got quite fast."

"Thank God," Carla murmured. She stepped closer, not bothering to pick up any of the remaining mess. She just stopped in front of Lisa, entirely invading her personal space. "They're exhausting. I usually kick them out hours ago."

"Why didn't you?" Lisa asked softly, her eyes dropping to the damp skin of Carla's collarbone before forcing them back up.

Carla tilted her head, a slow, heavy gaze sweeping over Lisa's face. "Because you were still here. And you seemed like you were finally having fun."

lLisa tensed up. They were standing inches apart. Neither of them said the words. Neither of them admitted what was actually happening, why Lisa had stayed for hours after she sobered up, or why Carla had spent the entire afternoon keeping Lisa firmly within arm's reach. They danced around the truth, terrified that speaking it aloud would make it real.

"I should..." Lisa started to say as her coice cracked. She swallowed hard. "I should probably head back."

Carla didn't step back to let her pass. Instead, she took a half step forward. Lisa’s back hit the edge of the patio table.

"The gate is right there, Lisa," Carla whispered, her voice getting low. "You can go whenever you want."

But Carla's body language was daring her to stay.

Carla reached up slowly. Lisa stopped breathing entirely. She thought Carla was going to touch her face, but instead, Carla’s fingers lightly grazed the edge of the towel over Lisa’s shoulder, adjusting it slightly. The movement brought Carla’s face close to hers .

Lisa could smell that intoxicating cologne. Carla’s eyes dropped to Lisa’s lips.

For one terrifying second, Lisa actually leaned in. Her body betrayed her.

Then, the reality of what she was doing snapped her back.

Becky. Betsy. Panic. She jerked backward so fast she nearly knocked the patio table over.

"I..I have to go," Lisa studdered her voice breathless and panicked.

Carla blinked. She took a step back, her expression shifting from desire to surprise. She was about to cross a line.

Lisa didn't wait for a response. She grabbed her sheer cover up off the chair, not even bothering to put it on, and practically sprinted toward the side gae.

"Goodnight, Lisa," Carla called out softly after her, her voice carrying easily through the dark yard.

Lisa didn't answer. She threw the wooden gate open, bolted across the grass to her own back door, and locked it behind her with trembling hands. She collapsed against the kitchen counter, burying her face in her hands. She was losing her mind. And worse, she was losing the fight.
