Chapter 8: Close

"I really need to shower," Becky sighed, stretching her arms over her head. "I have to be up so early tomorrow to help set up for that school function." She looked down at Lisa, who was leaning back against the edge of the pool. "Honey, do you mind staying out here and helping Carla clean up? The kids left floats and empty cans everywhere."

Lisa took the last swig of her beer. The tequila and alcohol were buzzing pleasantly in her veins, making her her head light now. "Yeah," she said, her voice a little raspy. "I'll help."

"Thanks, babe. See you inside," Becky smiled, grabbing her towel “Thanks Carla!” She added and walked out of the gate.

Carla didn't move. She stayed in the water, her arms resting on the edge of the pool, watching Lisa carefully.

The silence stretched out. Lisa sat on the concrete, the empty bottle resting on her knee, her chest rising and falling as she stared right back.

When Carla finally decided to move, she did it slowly. She pushed herself up onto the steps, the water cascading off her body. She walked right past Lisa.

There was plenty of room on the patio, but Carla deliberately stepped close. Her wet hip and thigh brushed right against Lisa's shoulder.

Lisa froze, her eyes tracking the movement, taking in the slow sway of Carla's hips as she bent down to pick up a bright pink pool float.

Carla didn't say a word, but as she stood up, she looked over her shoulder, catching Lisa's eye before slowly walking toward the small pool house at the back of the yard.

Lisa swallowed hard. The buzz from the alcohol was instantly replaced by adrenaline. She forced herself to stand up, her legs feeling a little unsteady.

She grabbed three empty seltzer cans in one hand and followed Carla across the concrete.

The storage closet attached to the pool house was a tight squeeze. It was packed to the ceiling with pool noodles and yard tools.

Carla was standing near the back, tossing the float onto a high shelf.

"Is there a trash can in here?" Lisa asked, her voice cracking. Her eyes immediately dropped to the water beading on Carla’s chest and stomach.

"Yeah, just there," Carla said quietly, pointing to a small plastic bin tucked into the back corner.

Lisa stepped forward, brushing past Carla in the narrow aisle. But as she did, Carla didn't step back.

Instead, Carla reached out. Her wet fingers grazed the bare skin of Lisa’s stomach, lightly tracing the defined lines of her abs.

Lisa froze instantly. The empty cans in her hand crinkled under her suddenly tight grip.

Carla didn't pull away. She stepped closer, her dark eyes locking onto Lisa's. She looked at Lisa's eyes, then dropped her gaze deliberately to her mouth.

Slowly, agonizingly, Carla dragged her index finger down the center of Lisa's stomach, stopping right at the very edge of her bikini bottoms.

Carla bit her bottom lip, her breathing suddenly nervous.

Lisa swallowed loudly. She couldn't take it anymore.

The tension snapped. Lisa shifted her weight, dropping the cans, and lightly pushed Carla backward until Carla's shoulders hit the wooden shelf.

Carla gasped softly, her heart pounding as she stared up at the detective.

They stood there in the dim light, breathing heavily, completely trapped by the gravity between them.

Lisa slowly brought her hand up, her fingers trembling just a little, and gently pushed a wet strand of dark hair out of Carla’s face.

"You're so beautiful," Lisa whispered, the confession slipping out before she could stop it.

A shy, completely genuine smile touched Carla's lips.

The confident, arrogant role melted away. Lisa let her fingers trail softly down Carla’s bare shoulder, and goosebumps instantly broke out across Carla’s skin.

Carla closed her eyes, leaning into the touch. "Lisa..." she whispered, her voice a desperate, breathless plea.

Lisa stepped into the space between them. She looked down at Carla's parted lips. She leaned in slowly, her hands dropping to grip Carla's hips, pulling her against her body. The heat between them was boiling.

"Mom!"

The loud yell from the yard was like abruptly.

Lisa and Carla jumped apart, their bodies separating. Lisa in absolute panic.

"Uh—yeah! In here!" Lisa yelled back, her voice cracking. She quickly bent down to pick up the dropped cans, desperately trying to cover up the reality of what was just about to happen.

Betsy appeared in the doorway of the shed, shielding her eyes from the setting sun. "Mom said for me to come get you. Your work phone and your cell phone won't stop ringing."

"Okay. Thanks, kiddo," Lisa said, her chest still heaving as she tried to slow her breathing.

Betsy nodded, completely oblivious and turned to walk back to the house.

Lisa moved further away. The reality of her life, her daughter, her wife, her demanding job, came rushing back, instantly sobering her up.

The warm, reckless buzz was gone, leaving nothing but a cold, heavy guilt.

She turned to look at Carla. Carla was leaning back against the shelf, her arms crossed defensively over her chest, the shy vulnerability entirely gone now.

"Duty calls," Carla said. Her tone was flat, but underneath it was a thick layer of sadness and deep frustration.

"Uh... thank you again for today," Lisa stammered, awkwardly holding the empty cans. She had no idea how to navigate the massive, dangerous line they had just crossed. She was terrified, but God, she was already regretting that she had to leave. "Do you need any more help in here?"

"No. Go ahead, Detective," Carla said quietly, not meeting her eyes. "I'll get this by myself."

Lisa hesitated for a fraction of a second before turning and walking out of the shed.

Carla stayed standing in the shed, listening to the gate close.

By myself, she thought, the words echoing bitterly in her head.

She picked up a stray pool noodle and threw it angrily into the corner.

It was exactly what she had always felt like. No matter who she brought home, no matter how hard she pushed, she was always, inevitably, by herself.

Carla locked the back glass door and stood in the kitchen. She could still feel Lisa’s hands gripping her hips. She could still feel the heat radiating off of her body, and the ghost of Lisa's breath against her lips.

She paced the length of the living room, running her hands through her damp, tangled hair.

The physical frustration was an ache, made a hundred times worse by the alcohol in her system.

She hated how easily Lisa could just walk away. She hated how Lisa could hit the panic button, slip her mask back on, and run back into the safety of her perfect family life, leaving Carla standing alone.

Always alone. She laughed at herself and said out loud “She’s fucking married Carla.”

Sleep was completely out of the question. Carla walked into her office.

She sat down at her desk and opened her laptop.

If Lisa was going to play with her head and run back to her wife, Carla was going to rewrite the story.

She needed an outlet for the adrenaline and sadness that was taking over her. She rested her fingers on the keys, took a deep breath, and started to type.

::::::::The jazz playing in the corner of the dive bar was a low, sultry hum, but Detective Beth Casey couldn't hear it. Her entire focus was narrowed down to the liquid in her glass, and the woman sitting entirely too close to her in the booth.

Erin Sutton smiled, signaling the bartender for another round. "You're off the clock, Detective. You can relax."

Beth took a slow, heavy drink of her bourbon. The alcohol was warm. She turned her head, her eyes catching the dim light as she looked at Erin."

Erin’s smile turned to a serious face. She reached across the small, sticky table, her manicured fingers brushing the back of Beth’s hand. The touch sent shivers up Beth’s arm.

Beth didn't pull her hand away. The whiskey had stripped away her excuses, her rules, and the suffocating guilt that usually kept her chained to her routine. She was so incredibly tired of fighting it.

"Take me home…" Beth murmured. The words were meant to be a challenge, but her voice came out as a rough and breathless that completely betrayed how desperate she actually was.

The ride to Erin’s place was a silent. They arrived at Erin’s.

Beth stepped over the threshold, the door clicking shut behind her.

Erin didn't even turn the lights on. She turned around, the moonlight the only light seen. She stepped directly into Beth’s space, walking the detective backward until Beth's shoulders hit the solid wood of the door.

"You're a long way from your jurisdiction, Detective," Erin whispered.

Beth’s heart pounded. The smell of Erin’s expensive, intoxicating perfume completely clouded her senses, drowning out every rational thought she had left. "I don't care."

Erin stepped closer, her body pressing flush against Beth’s. The physical contact was a shock to the system, a sudden, searing heat that made Beth gasp softly. Erin’s hands came up, resting lightly on the collar of Beth’s jacket before slowly, deliberately, sliding the zipper down.

"All that authority," Erin whispered, her lips on Beth's ear, sending a shiver straight down the detective's body. "All those rules you follow so perfectly. You're going to forget every single one of them tonight."

Beth closed her eyes, her head falling back against the door, completely surrendering to the absolute power in the other woman's hands. "Make me," Beth breathed.

Erin’s hands slid beneath shirt, her palms her hand grabbing Beth’s hips. She leaned in, her mouth hovering a fraction of an inch over Beth's, finally taking exactly what she wanted.

::::::::

Carla stopped typing. Her breathing was heavy, matching the detective in her story. She stared at the blinking cursor, the words blurring on the screen, the deep, aching heat in her stomach refusing to subside. She slammed the laptop shut, dropping her head into her hands. Fiction wasn't enough anymore.
